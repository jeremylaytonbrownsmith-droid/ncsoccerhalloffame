Privacy Policy

How we handle information collected through this website

Last updated November 2026

What We Collect

This website does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers of any kind. We only collect information you choose to give us directly, through:

  • The Contact Us form (name, email address, and your message)
  • Linked Google Forms used to submit attendee names for dinner tickets, or artwork and details for program ads
  • Ticket and ad purchases, which are processed entirely by Square, a third-party payment provider — we do not see or store your card information

How We Use It

Information you submit is used only to respond to your message, prepare seating and the event program, and recognize sponsors and advertisers in the induction dinner program. We do not sell or share your information with third parties, other than the services (Formspree, Google Forms, Square) that directly process the submission on our behalf.

Photos & Galleries

Photos from past induction ceremonies are hosted in Dropbox and may be shared with attendees and the public to celebrate our honorees. If you'd like a photo of yourself removed, contact us using the form above and we'll take care of it.

Questions

If you have questions about this policy or want to review, correct, or delete information you've submitted, contact us.
