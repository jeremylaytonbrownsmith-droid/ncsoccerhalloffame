About the HOF

Promote and support the game of soccer in the State of North Carolina; to recognize and honor those persons who have made significant contributions to the game of soccer in the State of North Carolina.

Our History

The North Carolina Soccer Hall of Fame was established in 1996, with its first class of inductees honored in 1997. Each year, the Hall of Fame celebrates the players, coaches, administrators, and contributors whose dedication has grown the game across North Carolina.
