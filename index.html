North Carolina Soccer Hall of Fame

Promote and support the game of soccer in the State of North Carolina; to recognize and honor those persons who have made significant contributions to the game of soccer in the State of North Carolina.

Congratulations to the Class of 2025
Inducted March 7, 2026

The North Carolina Soccer Hall of Fame celebrated the induction of four remarkable individuals whose dedication, vision, and leadership have shaped the growth of the game across the state. From building grassroots programs and guiding championship teams to advancing opportunities for women and high school players, these honorees represent the very best of North Carolina's soccer legacy.
